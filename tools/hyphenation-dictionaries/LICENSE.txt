Hyphenation Pattern Dictionaries
================================
The hyphenation pattern files in this directory are derived from the
upstream TeX hyph-utf8 and LibreOffice/Hunspell libhyphen repositories
(Knuth-Liang hyphenation patterns and non-standard spelling-change rules).
